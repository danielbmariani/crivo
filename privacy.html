Privacy policy

Effective October 27, 2026.

Crivo is an Apple TV app that recommends films and TV shows. It is built to work without collecting personal data. This page explains the little that happens with your data.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign up, email or password.
  • No collection of name, location, contacts or any personal data.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties.

What stays on your device

Your preferences are stored only on the Apple TV: country, selected streaming services, genre and year filters, and the list of titles you hid. None of it leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes everything.

Network connections

To show the catalog, the app sends requests to these services. The requests carry only the title being looked up and, for availability checks, the country you chose. As with any internet access, the service contacted sees the device IP address.

  • TMDB (themoviedb.org): metadata, posters and synopses. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
  • JustWatch: which streaming services carry each title in your country.
  • GitHub: the curated Crivo catalog (awards, festivals and canon lists) is downloaded from a public repository.

When you choose Watch, the app opens the corresponding streaming service app. From there, that service's privacy policy applies.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with its effective date.
